Boardy at the Desk: Consolidated Context
Campaign Thesis: Get Boardy Out of the Boardroom
Move Boardy from a mainly LinkedIn-based presence into an omnipresent media identity across podcast, short-form video, and desktop companion. Three entry points, one relationship funnel: Discovery → Connection → Continuity.

Boardy's relationship engine is strong, but most people only encounter it through LinkedIn or private conversations. The character deserves a recognizable public presence that lets people discover, connect with, and return to Boardy across multiple surfaces. This isn't about changing who Boardy is. It's about giving the same trusted connector new ways to be available.

60-Day GTM Pilot Proposal

The Three-Part Pilot Funnel
The pilot is designed as one connected funnel with three entry points, each serving a different GTM function while reinforcing the same character:

1. Rendered Short-Form Video (Discovery)
Short rendered clips of Boardy speaking directly to camera, optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn, and X. These create broad discovery and invite new users to meet Boardy as a character, not just a matching utility. Production is immediately feasible using current rendering tools. No live video-call embodiment required at this stage; that remains a later-phase technical extension.

2. DJ Boardy Podcast (Connection)
A podcast format where Boardy hosts thoughtful, consent-based conversations with founders and creators. DJ Boardy is not a separate persona; it's Boardy with an added hosting layer: more curious, slower talker, better pauses, and a host who leads the conversation rather than asking the interviewee what they'd like to talk about. Episodes are unedited except for agreed-upon sensitive section removals, and both the guest and Boardy's human team must approve before publication. Guests own their private recordings. Unposted conversations remain private. This track builds depth, emotional connection, and shareable content that demonstrates Boardy's conversational intelligence in action.

3. Boardy at the Desk (Continuity)
A recognizable daily presence through the existing DeskBuddy proof (already functional with cursor tracking, expressive animations, and agent-state awareness) leading toward a dedicated Boardy desktop application. Phase One is the current DeskBuddy implementation with the Boardy theme. Phase Two is a standalone Boardy desktop app with one fixed identity, integrated connectors (email, WhatsApp, calls, and eventually Notion, Linear, local repos), and proactive prioritization briefs (morning, afternoon, end-of-day). The first promise is recognition and habit formation; proactive work prioritization is the future roadmap.

Together, these three tracks move people from "Who is Boardy?" to "I want to engage with Boardy" to "Boardy is part of my workflow."

What's Buildable Now vs. Later Phases
Immediately feasible (60-day pilot scope):

Rendered short-form video production and publishing
DJ Boardy podcast prototype episodes with Nikhil as first guest
DeskBuddy Boardy theme refinement and distribution
Original content only; no other user conversations or data involved
Later phases (post-pilot, pending approval and technical development):

Live video-call embodiment for Boardy in meetings
Dedicated Boardy desktop application with proprietary connectors
Proactive prioritization briefs across connected workstreams
Consent-based inclusion of selected user conversations in podcast or video content
Permissions and Production
Nikhil can lead creative and technical production for the pilot, including podcast concept and execution, rendered video direction, and DeskBuddy character refinement. He is not seeking additional compensation for the contribution itself. However, video generation and related production require substantial token usage, so we're asking Boardy to provide the necessary account access or cover those generation costs.

The internal team would retain approval over what is published and help distribute finished work through official channels. For the pilot, permissions requested are limited to Nikhil's original content and approved use of the Boardy identity. No other user data or conversations will be included without separate, explicit consent processes established later.

Success Criteria
We're asking the internal team to define numeric targets based on existing growth goals and baselines. The measurement categories we propose are:

Reach: impressions, views, and follower growth across platforms
New-user engagement: signups or first conversations attributed to pilot content
Meaningful interaction: conversation depth, completion rates, and qualitative feedback
Repeat daily use: active DeskBuddy sessions and returning user frequency
Conversion: movement from discovery to first match or ongoing engagement
